03 / READ THE FINE PRINT

Good questions start with honest data.

Original data notes ↗
01

A consistent price rule.

All views include sales from $10,000 through $5,000,000, inclusive. Extreme prices are excluded, not replaced.

02

Missing means unknown.

Days on market = contract date − list date. Both dates are required; same-day contracts count as 0. Price per square foot requires positive living area. Each metric shows its own sample size.

03

Context before conclusions.

This is a historical collection, not a current listing feed or a complete census of a housing market. Partial years and a changing mix of homes limit comparisons.

More about the data, limitations, and calculations +

One listing, one record. We use listing_id as the key and retain repeated street addresses, which can represent different units or sales. ZIP codes and source identifiers stay as text.

Property descriptions have limits. The reported home_type is unreliable, so we do not classify or filter homes with it. Lot size, taxes, and extreme bathroom counts also contain errors and are not used in the calculations.

Metric definitions. The median is the middle value after sorting (the average of the two middle values for an even count). Median price per square foot is the median of individual sale_price / living_area_sqft ratios. Missing or zero living area is excluded only from that metric. Negative date intervals, if present, are excluded from days on market. Displayed dollar values are rounded to whole dollars after calculation.

Coverage varies. Homes without listing or contract dates can differ systematically from homes with those dates. Days on market describes only the recorded subset. Groups with fewer than 30 observations are marked “small sample”; that is a display caution, not a statistical significance test. Unknown sale dates appear in “Unknown date” and in “All years.” No inflation adjustment is applied.

Campus assignments. Each home is assigned to the nearest of the dataset's 11 campus landmarks using coordinates; a city's name does not define its campus. Distance groups are 0–1, over 1–3, over 3–5, over 5–10, and over 10 miles.

Source. College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Collected March 2026 from public property listings. Values are as reported by the collection source and were not verified against county records.
